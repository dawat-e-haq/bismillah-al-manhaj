📖 Fiqhul Hadeeth - فقہ الحدیث

Page 1 - Hadees
لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ

Yaqeenan tumahare liye Rasool Allah (ﷺ) mein Uswa-e-Hasanah maujood hai, her us shakhs ke liye jo Allah Ta‘ala ki taraf aur Qayamat ke din ki ummeed rakhta hai aur ba’kasrat Allah ko yaad karta hai.

(سورة الأحزاب: 21)
Page 2 - Hadees ki Tareef

Nabi ﷺ ki taraf mansoob qoul, amal, taqreer aur sifat ko hadees kehte hain.

Taqreer ka matlab yeh hai ke Nabi ﷺ ki maujoodgi mein kisi Sahabi ne koi kaam kiya ya koi baat kahi, aur Aap ﷺ ne us par tok ka nahi, to Aap ﷺ ki yeh khamoshi bhi hadees hai.

(Pg-1)
